What you are looking at

Every value here is computed by the same physics the built-in simulator uses: exponential coolant warm-up from ambient (18 °C) towards 92 °C, a soft rev limiter at 8500 rpm (the catalogued 5AM cut-off), idle around 1250 rpm, a charging system that holds 14.2 V, closed-loop lambda once warm. In the workstation these values are not a demo — they arrive as KWP2000 responses over K-Line or CAN, with the raw bytes kept next to every decoded value.

The safety rules are real too: try the fuel pump prime with the engine running, or clearing the fault memory without confirming. The workstation refuses exactly the same way, on purpose.
