# Code of conduct

This is a small project about a specific problem: making motorcycle
diagnostics honest and safe. Disagreement is welcome and expected. Contempt is
not.

## The short version

* **Argue about evidence, not about people.** "That decoder's source says X,
  not Y" is a contribution. "You clearly don't know what you're doing" is not.
* **Assume the other person owns the motorcycle in question.** People arrive
  here with a bike that is already broken. A terse "read the docs" costs them a
  day.
* **Safety claims get extra scrutiny, and that is not a personal attack.** If
  someone says your change could brick an ECU, the answer is the test or the
  source, not the volume.
* **Do not post someone's ECU image, VIN, log or identification block without
  their consent.** Those bytes identify a vehicle and a person.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, insults, discriminatory language, sexual attention, doxxing, and
publishing another person's private data or a vehicle's identifying bytes.
Sustained disruption of a technical discussion after being asked to stop.

## Scope

Issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages and anything else attached
to the project. The maintainer may edit, lock or remove contributions that
break these rules, and may block an account for repeated violations.

## Reporting

Report a problem privately to the maintainer through
[GitHub](https://github.com/ahardkore) or, for anything security-related, through
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Reports are read by the maintainer; you will get a
response and, where appropriate, an explanation of what was done.
