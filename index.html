Coverage & confidence

Many makes.
Specific capabilities.

GuzziOnBoard began with Moto Guzzi, but the workspace reaches beyond one badge. What you can do depends on the exact ECU, software and evidence behind each profile.

Read the coverage overview →
MarelliMoto Guzzi and selected Ducati / Aprilia applications, with profile-specific gates.
HondaOffline response decoding, XDF research and narrowly scoped offline builds.
Kawasaki / TriumphSelected read-only diagnostic paths, informed by third-party sources.
Harley-DavidsonRead-only J1850 diagnostics and offline capture / calibration-region research.
More researchSelected Suzuki, BMW, Yamaha and CFMoto offline tools and references.
ⓘBefore you connect: GuzziOnBoard's hardware integrations have not been validated on a physical motorcycle. The hosted demo is simulated; physical ECU writing remains blocked. A listed make or matching identifier is not proof of fitment.
