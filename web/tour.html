A quick look around

From garage to
workshop bench.

Explore the tools in GuzziOnBoard before connecting anything. The hosted workstation uses a simulated motorcycle; real diagnostic capabilities depend on the exact ECU profile.

Open the workstation demo ↗
Know the difference: A simulator result is not evidence of a successful physical connection or safe tune. GuzziOnBoard's hardware integrations have not been validated on a physical motorcycle, and physical ECU writing remains blocked.

The workspace

01 / GARAGE

Choose a starting point

Select a make, model and year, or use an available ECU-only profile. Review the resolved profile and its capabilities; a catalog match is not physical identification.

02 / DIAGNOSE

Observe and investigate

Use identification, live channels, fault views and guided tests where your selected profile and session permit them. Practise on the simulator first.

03 / MAPS

Inspect calibration data

Render and compare XDF tables against an image. The editor offers staged changes and research preview; compatibility, checksum and build gates still apply.

04 / TOOLS

Research and record

Compare ECU numbers, examine captures, calculate service intervals, search local manual text and review recordings. Source-specific tools explain their limits.
